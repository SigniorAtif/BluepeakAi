# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Owners of small and mid-sized businesses (bakeries, restaurants, clinics, salons, retailers,
industrial suppliers) in Vadodara and across India, plus some international clients. Most arrive from
a WhatsApp link, Instagram or a referral, on a phone, between customers. They are not technical; they
want to know what BluePeakAI builds, whether it has done it before, and how to start a conversation.
The first audience is the founder, Ayushi Shah, seeing the rebuild over a screen-shared call, so it
has to read as made for her business, not from a template.

## Product Purpose

The site explains five services (websites and landing pages, AI automation, WhatsApp systems, social
media, connected journeys), proves them with live client and demo sites, and turns a visitor into a
WhatsApp conversation or a project enquiry. Success is an enquiry from someone who opened a project
link first and trusted what they saw.

## Brand Personality

Practical, warm, considered. A founder-led studio, not a SaaS company: it speaks plainly, in the
business owner's terms, and never inflates. Calm surfaces with one accent signal; ambitious motion
only where it carries meaning (the dots in the hero are businesses and touchpoints, not decoration).

## Anti-references

- Their current site: stock SaaS blue on navy, icon-card grids, the fake "Growth OS" dashboard, the
  visible AI-written portfolio note.
- Template landing pages: every section the same kicker, two-tone headline, grey lede and list of
  rows; hero-metric blocks; identical icon cards.
- Gradient AI slop: glows, glassmorphism, gradient text, purple-blue haze.
- Invented proof: made-up stats, testimonials, client logos or claims not on their site.

## Design Principles

1. **Their words, trimmed.** Copy comes from bluepeakai.in; nothing is invented.
2. **Show the work.** Real projects, real screenshots, links that open the live site.
3. **Every section its own beat.** Sections differ in structure and pace; no repeated template.
4. **Quiet surfaces, one signal.** Restraint everywhere so the single accent means something.
5. **Spectacle earns its place.** Motion explains (scattered tools becoming one system) and is skippable; reduced motion gets a settled page.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA in light and dark themes and for every accent. `prefers-reduced-motion` always gets a
static, fully readable page. Keyboard reachable throughout (⌘K palette, visible focus rings). Phones
are first-class: no horizontal scroll, tap targets at least 44px, nothing hover-only.
